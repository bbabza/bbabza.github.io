Our Story

About Bezwada Bar Association

A Legacy of Legal Excellence

The Bezwada Bar Association was established in 1924 in the historic city of Vijayawada (Bezwada), on the banks of the Krishna River. Founded by a distinguished group of advocates committed to the rule of law and professional integrity, the association has served as the backbone of the legal community in Krishna District for over a century.

Through the decades of India's independence movement, post-independence legal reforms, and the formation of Andhra Pradesh, the Bezwada Bar Association has remained a steadfast guardian of the legal profession — advocating for judicial reforms, legal education, and the welfare of its members.

Today, with over 1,800 enrolled advocates, the association functions under the auspices of the District Court, Vijayawada, and maintains strong ties with the Andhra Pradesh High Court, the Bar Council of Andhra Pradesh, and the Bar Council of India.

⚖
Mission

To protect and promote the rights, welfare, and professional interests of advocates practicing in Vijayawada.

📌
Vision

A vibrant, inclusive legal community committed to access to justice, ethics, and the rule of law.

Our Journey

  • 1924
    Foundation

    Bezwada Bar Association formally constituted by leading advocates of the Krishna District.

  • 1947
    Post-Independence Era

    Association played an active role in shaping legal institutions in newly independent India.

  • 1956
    State Reorganisation

    Adapted to the formation of Andhra Pradesh; strengthened ties with the newly established AP High Court.

  • 1985
    New Premises

    Inaugurated the current Bar Association Hall adjacent to the District Court complex.

  • 2014
    State Bifurcation

    Navigated the reorganisation of Andhra Pradesh; continued to represent advocates' interests during transition.

  • 2024
    Centenary Year

    Celebrated 100 years of service to the legal fraternity with a grand centenary convention.
