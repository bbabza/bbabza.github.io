A Legacy of Legal Excellence

The Bezwada Bar Association was established in 1906 in the historic city of Vijayawada (Bezwada), on the banks of the Krishna River. Founded by a distinguished group of advocates committed to the rule of law and professional integrity, the association has served as the backbone of the legal community in Krishna District for over a century.

Through the decades of India's independence movement, post-independence legal reforms, and the formation of Andhra Pradesh, the Bezwada Bar Association has remained a steadfast guardian of the legal profession — advocating for judicial reforms, legal education, and the welfare of its members.

Today, with over 1,800 enrolled advocates, the association functions at the Civil Courts Campus, Gopala Reddy Road, Governorpet, Vijayawada, and maintains strong ties with the Andhra Pradesh High Court, the Bar Council of Andhra Pradesh, and the Bar Council of India.

Our Journey

  • 1906
    Foundation

    The Bezwada Bar Association formally constituted by leading advocates of the Krishna District.

  • 1947
    Post-Independence Era

    Association played an active role in shaping legal institutions in newly independent India.

  • 1956
    State Reorganisation

    Adapted to the formation of Andhra Pradesh; strengthened ties with the newly established AP High Court.

  • 1985
    Current Premises

    Shifted to the Civil Courts Campus, Gopala Reddy Road, Governorpet — the current premises.

  • 2014
    State Bifurcation

    Navigated the reorganisation of Andhra Pradesh; continued to represent advocates' interests during transition.

  • 2026
    Advocacy for Members

    The Governing Body unanimously resolved to abstain from duties in protest against police atrocities on advocates, reaffirming the Bar's commitment to protecting its members.
